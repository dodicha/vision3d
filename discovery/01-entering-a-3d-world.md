# Discovery 01

## Entering a 3D World

---

### Question

What makes a three-dimensional world perceivable?

---

### Observation 01

Space may exist without objects.

However, it becomes perceivable only when at least one object appears within it.

---

### Observation 02

Entering a space is the first step toward understanding it.

Without entering, interaction cannot begin.

---

### Observation 03

The first perception after entering is not the object.

It is the space itself.

Only then do objects begin to gain meaning.

---

### Observation 04

Perception begins when receiving information first becomes possible.

For this to happen, three conditions must exist:

- Information must exist.
- There must be a way for that information to become observable.
- There must be an observer capable of receiving it.

### Observation 05

New information appears when the relationship between the observer and the object changes.

---

### Observation 06

Movement alone does not create new information.

If the relationship remains unchanged, perception does not grow.

---

### Observation 07

Understanding is limited by the information accessible to the observer.

---

### Observation 08

An object is not limited by what the observer can perceive.

The observer's understanding is limited by the information they can access.
